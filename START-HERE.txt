PAGERWATCH 1.0.11 — by Nester

INSTALL FROM WINDOWS
1. Download PagerWatch-1.0.11-release.zip and extract it on your Windows PC.
2. Connect your PC to the Pager and open WinSCP.
3. Connect with SCP, host 172.16.52.1, port 22, username root and your
   Pager's root password. Use its actual IP if different.
4. Open /root/payloads/user/reconnaissance/ on the Pager.
5. Drag and drop the entire extracted PagerWatch folder into that directory.
6. Launch Payloads > User > Reconnaissance > PagerWatch. Press A.

Copy the whole folder, not just payload.sh or the ZIP.
Startup attempts to install missing supported dependencies when the Pager
has internet access. Compatible USB drivers are required.

DASHBOARD
Connect to Pager Wi-Fi. Open the HTTPS address on WEB and enter its code.
Default: https://172.16.52.1:8443

GPS
Phone GPS > Share this device's GPS. Allow location and wait for a fresh fix.
Keep the phone awake, connected and the page open.

PAGER BUTTONS
Left/Right: tabs. Up/Down: select/scroll. A: open/apply. B: back.
FIELD: run summary, GPS, known gear and saved sessions.
Device details > A: actions and device research.

UPDATE
Finish the payload, replace matching release files, keep config.json and
.state. Relaunch and refresh the dashboard.

RESULTS
Use Controls & exports in the dashboard or retrieve:
/mmc/root/loot/PagerWatch/
